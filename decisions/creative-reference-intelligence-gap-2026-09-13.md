## Incident

The system had a broad authorial-voice reference corpus containing technically strong and entertaining creators across multiple genres.

That corpus was then implicitly treated as if it were sufficient current reference intelligence for:
- selling websites/landing pages;
- CRM/automation services;
- commercial personal-brand content for non-technical business buyers.

This was a scope error.

The existing authorial corpus explicitly states that:
- it is not a ranking of best creators;
- it exists to map voice/delivery mechanisms;
- many references are outside the target commercial niche.

Therefore it cannot serve as the required benchmark for "how top service creators in this niche actually structure, record and sell through content."

## Root cause

Two-layer failure:

1. **REFERENCE-SCOPE MISMATCH**
   - authorial voice reference intelligence was reused outside its research contract.

2. **MISSING CREATIVE-BENCHMARK GATE**
   - applied orchestration did not require current domain-specific elite benchmark evidence before Social Content Creative generated a launch-level commercial script.

This is not evidence that generic authorial-voice research is useless.
It is evidence that it cannot substitute for niche creator benchmarking.

## Elite benchmark rule

"Top" must not mean:
- random large accounts;
- follower count only;
- one viral post;
- creators selected because the model already knows them;
- aesthetically impressive but commercially irrelevant creators.

A benchmark candidate should score strongly on several of:

1. **commercial-model fit**
   - actually sells services/products adjacent to the target offer;
2. **audience fit**
   - speaks to buyers or to a highly transferable service-business audience;
3. **current activity**
   - recent 2026 content exists;
4. **sustained distribution**
   - meaningful repeated audience scale/performance, not one isolated hit;
5. **operator credibility**
   - real agency/freelance/service practice, not commentary only;
6. **creative craft**
   - repeatable strong hooks, structure, delivery, proof or visual storytelling;
7. **conversion architecture**
   - content has a clear role in generating trust/demand, not views only;
8. **transferability**
   - mechanism can transfer to Ali without copying expression or requiring a different business model.

High precision outranks corpus size.

Default:
**5–8 strong references beat 30 mixed references.**

## Required benchmark packet

Before launch-level creative in a new commercial niche, Market Intelligence should provide:

- benchmark purpose;
- selection criteria;
- selected creators;
- direct vs adjacent classification;
- current evidence of activity/scale/fit;
- sampled recent artifacts;
- observable content mechanisms;
- commercial path from content to offer;
- what transfers;
- what must not transfer;
- uncertainty / source limitations.

A generic inspiration list does not pass.
