## Brief

Personal professional Instagram handle.

Open creative space explicitly authorized:
- no requirement to include Ali, prostoali, AI, profession, product category or work vocabulary;
- abstract phrase / imagined place / state is allowed;
- short-to-medium rather than a long sentence;
- should feel like an identity/signature, not a SaaS company, job title, motivational quote or generic tech handle;
- current preference territory includes odd physical places, thresholds, unfinished surfaces, after-hours architecture and dry micro-sentences.

Hard constraints:
- no unsupported credential/status implication;
- readable and reasonably transcribable when spoken;
- obvious exact active identity/brand collision is material;
- adjacent meanings, ordinary phrase usage, books/songs or unrelated category noise are soft context unless they dominate identity/confusion;
- Instagram exact username availability remains UNVERIFIED until checked in-product.

## Finalists

### 1. `@pastthelastdoor` — PRIMARY HYPOTHESIS

Why it survives:
- reads as a tiny scene rather than a brand descriptor;
- creates narrative tension without explaining itself;
- abstract enough to outlive any current profession or technology;
- spoken English is natural and the concatenated handle remains readable;
- no obvious exact dominant brand/public identity surfaced in current indexed screening.

Trade-offs:
- the phrase naturally occurs in ordinary prose/gaming directions;
- exact Instagram availability UNVERIFIED.

Professional judgment:
- strongest balance of strange + memorable + human + not overdesigned.

### 2. `@halflitlanding`

Why it survives:
- highly visual atmosphere in only three words;
- feels like a physical place rather than a startup;
- strong alliteration/rhythm;
- current exact indexed screening did not surface an obvious dominant identity.

Trade-offs:
- `landing` has multiple English meanings;
- when heard aloud, some users may initially parse `half-lit` differently;
- Instagram availability UNVERIFIED.

### 3. `@nosignoutside`

Why it survives:
- concise, natural and easy to type;
- creates an unmarked-door / hidden-place image without overexplaining;
- ordinary phrase use is not treated as brand collision for this personal-handle brief.

Trade-offs:
- the phrase is commonly used descriptively for restaurants/bars/places with no signage, so semantic uniqueness is lower;
- Instagram availability UNVERIFIED.

### 4. `@lastlightupstairs`

Why it survives:
- cinematic and slightly more poetic than the top three;
- still reads as a place/state rather than a profession;
- no obvious exact dominant identity surfaced in current indexed screening.

Trade-offs:
- longer;
- more lyrical, which may be either strength or excess depending on desired tone;
- Instagram availability UNVERIFIED.

### 5. `@undertheprimer`

Why it remains:
- strong established candidate from the prior naming run;
- compact and metaphorically rich;
- exact identity collision was not established.

Trade-offs:
- substantial ordinary semantic noise from cosmetics and coatings/construction;
- user already judged that noise tolerable for a personal handle;
- Instagram availability UNVERIFIED.

### 6. `@stillcuring`

Why it remains:
- short and natural;
- unfinished-state metaphor is immediately legible.

Trade-offs:
- ordinary usage spans concrete/resin/nails/food/other curing contexts;
- less spatial/cinematic character than the new leaders;
- Instagram availability UNVERIFIED.

## Screened down / rejected examples

- `The Unlisted Floor`: strong concept, but current 2026 exact-title use exists in fiction/audiobook contexts; soft creative-title collision plus slightly more horror-coded than desired.
- `After The Last Door`: exact 2026 music-title use surfaced; retained only as evidence that `Past The Last Door` is the cleaner construction.
- `Take Back Stairs`: concatenated/omitted-article reading creates avoidable semantic ambiguity.
- `Nothing Upstairs` / `Nobody Upstairs`: English idiomatic connotation can imply stupidity / "nothing going on upstairs"; rejected on unintended meaning rather than market collision.
- `The Quiet Machinery`: active 2026 music/fiction identity/title use; reject from current shortlist.
