## Current market intelligence

Live review on 2026-09-11 confirms that current AI/SaaS branding still frequently uses:
- dark fields;
- geometric modular marks;
- interlocking/stacked structures;
- negative-space channels;
- cube/hex and layered system metaphors.

Relevant current examples include:
- Automo (2026): modular interconnected nodes for workflow/integration.
- NUON (2026): two geometric shapes on a dark field separated by a diagonal negative-space channel.
- STRATA (2026): modular horizontal layers representing coordinated systems.
- ONEX AI (2026): dark-mode geometric AI/SaaS identity.

The candidate therefore remains inside a recognizable technology/system visual language, but its current asymmetric composition and role-differentiated modules no longer reproduce the major generic mechanisms previously rejected.

## Residual risk

The mark still uses broad geometric/monochrome tech language. This is acceptable at this gate because:
- the exact structural mechanism is no longer a common letter/cube/rotor shorthand;
- the asymmetric joint is specific enough to provide authored identity;
- the positioning fit is achieved through construction logic rather than literal AI decoration.

This is not trademark/legal clearance and does not prove global uniqueness.

## Decision

**FLAT GEOMETRY FROZEN.**

Do not continue flat-shape exploration unless a later collision or materially stronger evidence appears.

Next authorized creative step:
- re-apply restrained 3D/material treatment to this exact frozen geometry;
- 3D must remain presentation/finish, not alter the structural silhouette;
- then perform one final rendered QC for small-size readability, material restraint, anti-generic-AI risk and fidelity to the frozen geometry.

## Locks

1. Preserve the current outer silhouette and central interface relationship.

4. No radial symmetry or pinwheel rotation.

6. No handle/initial dependency.
