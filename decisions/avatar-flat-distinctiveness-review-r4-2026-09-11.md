Current flat black/white avatar candidate with three rounded asymmetric modules around a central negative space.

Protected thesis:
**Real business problems -> working systems.**

## Render observation

Observed at 64 px and 32 px.

- Readability remains strong.
- Previous S-letter and cube/hex readings are removed.
- The first-read is now a three-blade / aperture / pinwheel / rotational-system mark.
- At small size, the construction logic becomes less legible as "modules forming a working system" and more like motion/rotation around a center.

Live review 2026-09-11 found materially similar visual mechanisms already used in AI/tech branding:
- MultiOn AI uses three interlocking white curved shapes in triangular rotation on a black field.
- Current AI/SaaS logo work repeatedly uses three-part rotational/interlocking geometry and central negative space to signal automation, systems, movement and AI.
- Arize branding uses three rising segments in rotational symmetry to encode a connected AI system.

## Root cause

CONCEPT-SHAPE convergence.

The latest repair escaped the previous generic mechanisms, but converged on another established tech/AI mechanism: three-part rotational symmetry around a central triangular/negative-space core.

The mark now communicates motion/loop/rotation more strongly than construction/assembly.

## Decision

**DIRECTION REVISE — DO NOT RETURN TO 3D YET.**

Preserve:
- three-module logic;
- monochrome small-size discipline;
- no initials/letters;
- no cube/hex container;
- no literal AI symbols.

Change:
1. remove three-way rotational symmetry;
2. avoid radial/pinwheel/aperture composition;
3. use clearly unequal module roles rather than three visually equivalent blades;
4. create one directional assembly relationship: source -> interface/joint -> assembled outcome, or primary block + secondary connector + tertiary support;
5. retain an asymmetric signature joint that survives 32/64 px;
6. reject any form that first reads as aperture, pinwheel, recycling loop, rotor, flower, camera shutter, or generic three-part tech mark.

## Revisit trigger

Freeze geometry only when the flat mark retains systems-building meaning at small size without first-reading as a letter, cube/hex, folded ribbon, impossible box, aperture/pinwheel, or generic rotational tech symbol.
