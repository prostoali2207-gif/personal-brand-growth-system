## Purpose

Resolve disagreement between prior self-selection and broader visual judgment by reviewing anonymized avatar candidates without provenance labels or creator history.

## Method

Five finalists were anonymized as A-E and compared at:
- profile-scale preview;
- 52 px;
- 32 px.

Fixed criteria:
- memorability / signature value — 25%
- small-size viability — 20%
- fit to evidence-backed brand personality — 20%
- distinctiveness — 15%
- unintended-association resistance — 10%
- generic-tech / AI / crypto risk resistance — 10%

Brand constraints:
- practice-led systems builder;
- precise, practical, curious, direct, human;
- avoid guru/corporate-premium theater;
- avoid generic AI/cyberpunk/crypto language;
- no dependence on handle/initials.

## Blind scores

| Candidate | Weighted score / 100 | Review summary |
|---|---:|---|
| A | 77.0 | strongest compact badge-read and memorability; penalized for shield/crypto association |
| B | 75.25 | strong signature negative space; penalized for cold corporate-tech / metallic severity and letter-like read |
| C | 73.25 | strongest systems/modularity semantics; penalized heavily for small-size complexity |
| D | **85.0** | best balance of signature negative space, brand-fit, distinctiveness and 32–52 px viability |
| E | 77.5 | strongest simplicity/small-size safety; penalized for being too safe and less memorable/distinctive |

## Verdict

**WINNER: D**

The previous self-review that selected E is not upheld by blind independent review.

D wins because it preserves:
- two-module logic;
- strong negative-space signature;
- warm human/non-corporate palette;
- good 52/32 px recognition;
- stronger memorability than E;
- lower shield/crypto risk than A;
- lower corporate-tech severity than B;
- much lower complexity than C.

## Important finding

The previous self-selection over-weighted compliance/safety relative to signature value and memorability.

This single case is evidence of a process weakness in self-review, not yet proof that the professional core itself requires repair.

## Next action

Do not modify the core yet.

Use D as the selected design direction and run one final production/QC pass:
1. normalize safe area;
2. verify 52/32 px after export;
3. verify actual Instagram crop;
4. only then update source-of-truth/current-avatar.md.

If repeated blind reviews continue to show the same self-review bias, escalate to Agent Architect as evidence for targeted evaluation/repair of the Visual Design / Art Direction candidate.
