At small avatar scale (~64 px):
- the mark collapses very clearly into a stylized **S**;
- the accidental letter read becomes stronger, not weaker;
- identity depends substantially on 3D lighting/material depth.

## Root cause

CONCEPT / SIGNATURE-STRUCTURE failure.

The repair increased visual impact, but the new geometry converged on an S-monogram mechanism. That violates the active constraint that the avatar must not derive identity from an unapproved handle/initial and weakens distinctiveness inside the AI/automation category.

## Decision

**RENDER REVISE — DO NOT FREEZE AS FINAL.**

Preserve:
- three-layer systems construction;
- compact dark-avatar format;
- strong depth and architectural precision;
- non-literal AI treatment.

Change only:
1. break the continuous top-middle-bottom S path;
2. create one asymmetric signature interruption/joint that reads as system assembly rather than a letter;
3. verify first in flat black/white at 64 px;
4. add 3D/material treatment only after the flat silhouette passes;
5. reject any repaired form that reads first as S, Z, E, C, cube, folded ribbon or generic hex-tech mark.

## Revisit trigger

One repaired silhouette that passes the accidental-letter and category-distinctiveness gates can be frozen as FINAL without reopening the broader concept.
