## Portfolio objective

Show the range of real services **across the batch**, without implying that every service depends on the others.

## Portfolio rule

One asset should normally have one primary commercial job.

Do not add another service merely to prove "depth."

Integrated service content is reserved for cases where the integration itself is the useful subject.

## Initial batch hypothesis

### 1. PARTS-LANDING-01 — Landing-specific product judgment
Source:
auto-parts landing.

Primary service:
**Website / Landing Page only.**

Evidence angle:
the request experience supports real auto-parts request structure such as vehicle context, multiple parts and photos.

Communication job:
show one thoughtful landing/form decision from a real project.

Explicit exclusion:
do not mention BayerCRM merely to show broader capability.

### 2. BAYER-CRM-01 — CRM-specific workflow judgment
Source:
BayerCRM.

Primary service:
**CRM only.**

Evidence angle:
mobile-first daily work, request status / next action, minimal fields/clicks.

Communication job:
show what makes a CRM useful in actual daily work.

Explicit exclusion:
do not introduce a landing unless it is necessary to the chosen case.

### 3. FLEET-AUTO-01 — Automation-specific rule
Source:
FleetDesk.

Primary service:
**Automation / operational system behavior.**

Evidence:
known contract/vehicle-state relationship.

Communication job:
show one rule that the system handles automatically.

Explicit exclusion:
do not turn it into a website or CRM pitch.

### 4. CUSTOM-SYSTEM-01 — When a custom internal tool is justified
Source:
FleetDesk and/or BayerCRM with exact evidence.

Primary service:
**Custom system.**

Communication job:
show a real workflow that needs specific states/rules rather than generic software.

### 5. INTEGRATION-01 — Integrated case, later
Source:
auto-parts landing + BayerCRM.

Primary job:
**Integration case**.

Only here is the landing -> CRM connection itself the subject.

This proves combinability without redefining either standalone service.

### 6. AI-METHOD-01 — Secondary technical credibility
Source:
professional-ai-agents.

Role:
secondary/technical audience.
Not required to establish the client-facing service portfolio.

## Why PARTS-LANDING-01 first

Not because landing is required to lead into other services.

It is selected as the first commercial case because:
- the user explicitly intends to sell landing work;
- a landing is easy for a non-technical business owner to recognize;
- current real project evidence can support a visible, bounded landing-specific decision;
- it allows the first asset to stay simple.

This does not make landing the permanent primary service.

## Sequence logic

`Landing proof -> CRM proof -> Automation proof -> Custom-system proof -> Integrated case when useful`

This is a **content-sequencing hypothesis**, not a service dependency map.

No buyer is expected to purchase in this order.
