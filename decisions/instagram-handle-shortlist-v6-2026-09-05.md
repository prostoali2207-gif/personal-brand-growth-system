## Why naming was reopened

The prior selected handle `@nosignoutside` survived memorability and transcription checks but failed a newly explicit personal-brand first-read test: it can read more naturally as a venue, room, studio, project or community than as a person/alias.

The user also identified a broader creative failure mode in earlier pools: many names felt visibly designed to look unusual. Brand Naming Practitioner v0.2.1 now treats performative cleverness and ceremonial over-analysis as defects.

## Brief

Target:
- professional personal Instagram identity;
- must feel attached to a real person rather than a company, lab, room, project or invented persona;
- should remain broad enough for real work across software, systems, automation, AI agents, CRM/SaaS, landing/product work and future adjacent work;
- no unsupported expert/credential claim;
- no requirement to include a profession/AI keyword;
- no requirement to avoid the name Ali;
- naturalness and durability matter more than novelty;
- simple is allowed to win.

## Identity-mode decision

Open exploration considered name-led, person-alias and concept-led modes.

Current professional judgment:
- **name-led is strongest for this brief**;
- not because `Ali` is mandatory;
- because current concept-led candidates repeatedly introduced place/project/constructed-persona ambiguity without compensating value.

## Rejected / screened down

- `@justali`: natural, but an established current creator already uses the JustAli identity at significant scale; unnecessary identity crowding.
- `@iamali`: very clear person identity but heavily reused across public profiles/handles and entities; too crowded to justify over cleaner options.
- `@withali`: reads more like a series, podcast, course or program “with Ali” than the person himself; active `withali` profile usage also exists.
- `@madebyali`: creator/brand/byline semantics rather than person identity.
- new abstract micro-sentences/place metaphors: rejected at territory level unless they materially outperform the simple name-led candidates; none did in this run.
