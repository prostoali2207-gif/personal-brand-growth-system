## Decision

Launch commercial short-form content as:

**Russian-first / English-accessible**

Operational meaning:
- spoken narration: Russian;
- primary spoken script: Russian;
- commercial terminology: ordinary buyer language, not developer jargon;
- English subtitles on commercially important short-form content;
- profile/service packaging should remain understandable to an English-speaking UAE prospect;
- do not mix Russian and English sentence-by-sentence merely to appear bilingual.

## Why

### Russian-first
- preserves the creator's strongest natural spoken register for early content;
- reduces performance friction while the narration/authorial system is still being established;
- allows clearer trust and nuance than forcing English-first before there is evidence that English delivery is equally strong.

### English-accessible
- avoids closing the account to the wider UAE business environment;
- lets English-speaking prospects understand the commercial meaning;
- preserves a future path to English-first or bilingual content if first-party signals justify it.

## Not decided

This is not a permanent language identity.

Do not infer:
- that the whole addressable market is Russian-speaking;
- that English-first will never be used;
- that every post requires two full spoken versions.

## Revisit triggers

Re-evaluate when:
- enough commercial content has been published to observe language-specific inbound;
- English-speaking prospects repeatedly engage or enquire;
- the creator's English spoken delivery becomes strong enough for direct comparison;
- a specific offer/channel requires English-first communication.
