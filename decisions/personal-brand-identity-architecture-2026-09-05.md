## Decision

Do not force the Instagram @username to function as the entire personal-brand name.

Recommended identity architecture:

**Public personal brand identity = Ali**

Instagram `@username` = platform address / locator, selected for:
- availability;
- readability;
- transcription;
- portability;
- low confusion;
- enough distinctiveness for account discovery.

The handle does not need to carry the whole brand story.

## Why

Previous naming cycles repeatedly over-loaded the handle:
- abstract phrase as identity;
- place/project metaphors;
- profession/role implications;
- performative unusualness;
- excessive explanation.

For a real-person professional brand, the strongest current path is person-first identity with a clean handle.
