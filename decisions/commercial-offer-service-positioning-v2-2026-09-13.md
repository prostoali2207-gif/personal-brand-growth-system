## Business objective

Attract ordinary business clients for practical digital work:
- websites / landing pages;
- CRM systems;
- business automation / integrations;
- custom internal systems where needed.

AI remains an implementation capability / optional specialist layer, not the default buyer-facing category.

## Core decision

Use a **modular service portfolio**, not a mandatory service ladder.

### Module A — Website / Landing Page

A complete standalone service.

Possible buyer jobs include:
- present an offer/business clearly;
- create a page for a campaign/service;
- provide a clear contact/request path;
- build a useful digital front door.

A landing does **not** require a CRM connection to be a legitimate finished service.

Do not promise conversion, leads, SEO or revenue without evidence.

### Module B — CRM

A complete standalone service when the business needs:
- request/customer tracking;
- statuses / next actions;
- a shared working view;
- workflow visibility.

A CRM does not require a new website.

### Module C — Business Automation / Integrations

A complete standalone service when a known repetitive handoff/rule should run automatically between existing tools/processes.

Do not force a website or CRM rebuild when the automation problem can be solved independently.

### Module D — Custom Internal System / Custom CRM

Use when the workflow requires specific roles, states, documents, calculations or rules that justify a purpose-built tool.

This can be a standalone engagement.

### Optional capability — AI

AI may be:
- implementation method;
- automation mechanism;
- specialist solution;
- technical credibility proof.

It is not automatically a separate front-door service and must not be inserted when unnecessary.

## Composition rule

Modules may be combined **only when the buyer problem or real project requires it**.

Valid examples:
- landing only;
- CRM only;
- automation between existing tools;
- custom system only;
- landing + CRM;
- CRM + automation;
- website + automation;
- larger integrated system.

No arrow between modules implies obligation.

## Buyer-facing taxonomy

`Website / Landing | CRM | Automation / Integrations | Custom Systems`

This is a set of service categories, not a funnel.

## Brand/content implication

Do not make every content asset prove breadth.

Instead:
- a landing asset should be allowed to stay about landing work;
- a CRM asset should be allowed to stay about CRM;
- an automation asset should be allowed to stay about automation;
- integrated cases should be labeled/treated as integrated cases.

Portfolio breadth emerges across multiple assets.

## Commercial hypothesis

A modular service presentation will create less category confusion than a ladder that implies every landing naturally expands into CRM/automation.

This remains a hypothesis until first-party commercial signals exist.

## Unresolved
