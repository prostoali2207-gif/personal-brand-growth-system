## CORE JUDGMENT — positioning specialization

### Selected identity thesis

**Real business problems -> working systems.**

Expanded form:

**The personal brand should show how real operational problems are turned into working software, automations and professional AI-agent systems — including the architecture, decisions, failures, repairs and iterations behind the work.**

### Niche / category

Primary:
- applied AI systems;
- business software / operational systems;
- automation;
- CRM / SaaS;
- professional AI-agent engineering.

Unifying lens:
- **systems building from real work**, not commentary about AI as a topic.

### Identity mode

**Practice-led / systems-builder identity.**

This is intentionally not:
- AI-news account;
- generic AI educator;
- prompt account;
- "make money with AI" identity;
- unsupported expert/guru identity;
- generic software-developer portfolio detached from business operations.

### Content-source rule

Primary content must originate from verified work:
- what is being built;
- why a decision was made;
- architecture and workflow;
- failures and root causes;
- evaluation and repair;
- what worked / did not work;
- real product and operational trade-offs.

No project may be turned into a stronger first-person authorship, client, revenue or performance claim without an approved `brand-claims/` record.

## Visual-identity translation

The visual system should communicate:
- modularity;
- construction;
- systems / interconnection;
- controlled transformation;
- operational precision;
- work-in-progress / iterative refinement when useful.

It should not depend on:
- the current handle or initials;
- brains, robots, circuit-heads or literal "AI" glyphs;
- neon/cyberpunk AI clichés;
- fake code/data symbolism;
- generic black-gold "premium AI" language;
- arbitrary abstract marks that need a retrofitted story.

### Avatar-specific constraint

A candidate avatar does **not** need to literally depict software, CRM or AI. It must instead fit the systems-building thesis through its structural logic and remain distinctive at small profile size.

The current layered/interlocking-monolith direction is therefore **conceptually compatible** with the niche because it can express modular system construction. It is not automatically approved: Visual Design / Art Direction still owns rendered distinctiveness, avatar-read, anti-generic-AI and final visual critique.

## Alternatives considered

### "AI expert"
REJECT.
- unsupported authority claim;
- too broad;
- collapses real project evidence into a title.

### "AI-agent builder"
REJECT as the whole identity.
- professional-agent engineering is real evidence, but it excludes FleetDesk, CRM/SaaS, automation and product-system work.

### "Software developer"
REJECT as primary positioning.
- too implementation-centric;
- does not capture business workflow, automation, product decisions and professional-agent system design.

### "Founder / entrepreneur"
REJECT as primary positioning.
- unnecessary for the evidence-backed content mechanism;
- would require separate support for the intended public claim.

### "Build in public"
REJECT as the niche.
- useful content mechanism, not the professional subject itself.

grounds:

status: open

## Constraints / locks created

1. Avatar and visual-identity work must route through this thesis while this decision is active.
2. Naming remains independent and reopened; no visual concept may pretend a handle is final.
3. Do not add literal AI symbolism merely to make the niche obvious.
4. Do not retrofit arbitrary visual forms with unsupported narrative.
5. Public first-person claims remain governed by `brand-claims/`.
6. Candidate professional cores retain candidate status; this specialization does not qualify them.

## Revisit trigger

Revisit when:
- materially new project evidence changes the dominant work pattern;
- audience/market research demonstrates a different useful category boundary;
- repeated content/performance evidence shows the thesis is misunderstood or attracts the wrong audience;
- the upstream Strategy candidate or Personal Brand specialization changes materially.
