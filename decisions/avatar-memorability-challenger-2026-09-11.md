## Trigger

A comparison avatar produced in a contaminated/non-independent run appeared more immediately memorable than the current final avatar.

This observation is accepted as a useful HYPOTHESIS, not as proof of superior brand performance.

## Evidence

Observed comparison:
- comparison mark has two dominant visual masses and one strong central negative-space gesture;
- current final avatar has three dominant modules plus a smaller interface event;
- at first glance the comparison mark therefore has lower reconstruction complexity.

Deterministic image proxy on the two supplied renders:
- current avatar: 3 dominant bright connected components; approx. 25 simplified contour vertices;
- comparison: 2 dominant bright connected components; approx. 15 simplified contour vertices.

These proxies support lower visual complexity only. They do NOT directly measure human memory or brand recall.

## Professional judgment

Current avatar remains stronger on:
- no accidental letter dependency;
- authored system-assembly logic;
- category distinctiveness;
- evidence-backed systems-builder fit.

Comparison mark appears stronger on:
- immediate gestalt;
- ease of mental reconstruction;
- first-glance memorability.

The comparison mark itself is NOT a valid replacement because its strong S-like read and familiar two-part tech-symbol grammar reduce ownability.

## Classification

GAP type:
**existing Visual Design capability / REFINE**.

No new core, specialization or profession required.

Failure is not established in production.
This is an optimization hypothesis.

## Decision

Do NOT reopen or replace the current FINAL avatar.

Create an isolated flat challenger with the narrow objective:

**increase immediate memorability while preserving the current identity constraints.**

### Challenger contract

Preserve:
- systems-builder structural logic;
- asymmetry;
- one authored interface/joint;
- no naming/initial dependency;
- no S/Z/E/C read;
- no cube/hex;
- no radial/pinwheel;
- no literal AI symbol.

Change:
- reduce number of competing visual events;
- create one dominant silhouette hierarchy;
- enlarge/emphasize one signature negative-space joint;
- reduce contour complexity;
- make the mark reconstructable from memory in one short verbal description.

### Evaluation

Challenger must beat current avatar on:
- immediate gestalt;
- silhouette economy;
- recall/reconstruction plausibility;

without losing:
- distinctiveness;
- systems-builder fit;
- small-size viability;
- personality fit;
- non-letter independence.

Production avatar remains authoritative until challenger clearly wins the full gate.
