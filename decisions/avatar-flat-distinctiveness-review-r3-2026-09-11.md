## Input

Current flat black/white avatar candidate derived from the layered/interlocking systems concept.

Protected thesis:
**Real business problems -> working systems.**

## Render observation

Observed at 64 px and 32 px.

- Small-size legibility is strong.
- The previous S-letter reading is largely removed.
- The dominant first-read is now an isometric cube / hexagonal tech container with separated internal planes.
- At 32 px, the form simplifies further into a familiar cube/box-tech silhouette.

## Current category evidence

Live review on 2026-09-11 shows cube/interlocking geometric marks remain common in current AI/SaaS/tech branding:
- Cubic identity references explicitly use cube/3D geometry for AI/SaaS.
- BoxBuild uses an impossible-cube icon for a SaaS/software-development platform.
- Current for-sale/interlocking tech marks explicitly position cube/interlocking geometry for tech startups and digital identities.
- Current SaaS logo guidance/examples continue to use stacked cube geometry to signal modularity, workflows and systems.

## Root cause

CONCEPT-SHAPE convergence.

Removing the S path exposed a second generic mechanism: isometric cube/hex-tech framing. The systems-building idea remains valid, but the outer container geometry is still too category-conventional.

## Decision

**DIRECTION REVISE — DO NOT RETURN TO 3D YET.**

Preserve:
- three-part modular system;
- black/white small-size discipline;
- asymmetry and disconnected modules;
- no literal AI symbols;
- no handle/initial dependency.

Change:
1. abandon closed or implied cube/hex outer boundary;
2. stop using isometric-box perspective as the organizing skeleton;
3. keep three modules but arrange them around an asymmetric non-container negative space;
4. require one unmistakable signature joint/interruption;
5. test first at 64 px and 32 px before any material/light treatment.

## Revisit trigger

Freeze geometry only when the flat mark no longer first-reads as a letter, cube, hexagon, folded ribbon, impossible box or generic tech container.
