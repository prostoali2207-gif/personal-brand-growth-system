## Root cause

Classification: IMPLEMENTATION / PRESENTATION LAYER.

The amber warmth improved the emotional temperature, but the combination of:
- polished metallic/ceramic surfaces;
- deep black background;
- cinematic edge lighting;
- near-product-shot finish

still reads as high-end tech / luxury AI branding.

The render therefore remains more monumental and premium than the active personality contract permits.

## Decision

Do not reopen or redesign the mark.

Keep locked:
- silhouette;
- three-module construction;
- signature joint;
- proportions;
- spacing/negative-space logic.

Revise only:
- material;
- lighting;
- background/value environment.

## Next treatment target

Create a less cinematic and less luxurious presentation:
- matte/tactile rather than polished metal;
- softer, more ambient light;
- charcoal / warm-neutral environment rather than black abyss;
- minimal or no glowing rim;
- keep precision through geometry, not prestige effects;
- retain enough contrast for 32/64 px.

Target perception:
**precise + practical + curious + human**, not premium-tech.
