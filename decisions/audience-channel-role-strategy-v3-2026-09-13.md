## Primary audience

Owners, founders, managers and digitally responsible staff in small-to-mid-sized UAE businesses who need a practical digital result and are not required to be technical.

## Independent buyer situations

### Website / Landing
"I need a site/landing page for the business, service, product or campaign."

This situation does not imply CRM need.

### CRM
"I need one place to manage requests/customers/statuses/next actions."

This situation does not imply website need.

### Automation / Integrations
"We keep repeating the same handoff/action between tools or people."

This situation does not imply website/CRM rebuild.

### Custom System
"Our workflow has specific rules/states/roles that off-the-shelf tools do not fit well."

This situation does not imply any fixed preceding service.

## Content relevance rule

For each asset ask:
**Which single buyer situation is this primarily helping the viewer understand?**

A second service may appear only when it is materially part of the real case.

Do not insert adjacent capability simply to signal breadth.

## Language

Russian-first / English-accessible remains active:
- spoken commercial short-form: Russian;
- English subtitles on commercially important content;
- English-readable profile/service packaging.

## AI

AI remains secondary capability/technical credibility unless a specific buyer problem makes it primary.

## Channel roles

Instagram:
discovery + trust + real-work commercial relevance.

LinkedIn:
deeper B2B reasoning/project decisions.

TikTok:
discovery test from proven mechanisms.

GitHub:
proof substrate, not buyer-facing explanation.

## Downstream lock

Content Portfolio must treat services as independent modules.
Breadth belongs to the portfolio, not every asset.
