## Decision question

What should the first coherent group of Personal Brand content communicate before individual assets are optimized, so that first-contact viewers do not mistake one project or one industry for the whole professional category?

## FACT / EVIDENCE

Verified work currently spans:
- FleetDesk — production rental-operations SaaS with operational workflows and automation;
- BayerCRM — mobile-first CRM/product-system work;
- auto-parts landing — landing -> Edge Function -> CRM request pipeline plus product/visual work;
- professional-ai-agents — professional AI-agent architecture, evaluation and qualification.

The repeated evidence pattern is:
`real operational problem -> explicit workflow/rule -> working software/automation/agent system -> verification`.

Automotive/mobility is the strongest current evidence wedge, but the active strategy explicitly states that it is not the permanent category boundary.

No public claim of sole authorship, revenue, savings, customer count or business-performance impact is approved here.

## CORE JUDGMENT — strategy candidate

### First-contact perception to establish

After the first coherent exposure, a viewer should be more likely to understand the work as:

**business systems / CRM / automation / integrated product work / professional AI-agent systems built around real operational problems**

and not primarily as:
- FleetDesk promotion;
- rent-a-car software;
- automotive-only content;
- generic AI news/tutorials;
- a generic website-design account.

This is a provisional strategy judgment from a CANDIDATE / NOT QUALIFIED strategy composition.

## Launch asset role

### LAUNCH-01 — CATEGORY ESTABLISHMENT / BREADTH WITH PROOF

Use **multiple real project proofs under one systems-building idea**.

This is specifically NOT:
- a services list;
- a portfolio montage with no argument;
- "I make websites, CRM, automation, AI";
- an unsupported authorship reel.

Required proof families:
1. **Landing / integration proof** — auto-parts landing -> Edge Function -> BayerCRM request path.
2. **Operational automation proof** — FleetDesk contract/vehicle state logic or another verified operational rule.
3. **AI-system proof** — professional-ai-agents lifecycle/evaluation/qualification evidence.

Communication job:
show that different visible outputs — landing, CRM/SaaS workflow, AI-agent system — belong to one professional pattern: real business logic is translated into explicit working systems.

Misclassification guard:
no one project may visually or verbally dominate enough that the asset reads as an advertisement for that product.

## Why this sequence

1. Breadth is established before depth.
2. FleetDesk can then be narrow without defining the whole category.
3. AI-agent engineering appears after the business-systems category is clear, reducing generic-AI-account risk.
4. CRM/product judgment broadens the evidence base again and prevents automation from becoming the only perceived skill.
