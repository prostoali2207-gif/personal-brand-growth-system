## Trigger

Issue #302 covers authorial script/persona craft. It does not establish competence in actual vocal performance.

## FACT / EVIDENCE

Current composition:
- Social Content Creative 0.1.0 owns exact script/copy.
- Authorial-voice v0.2 candidate overlay from issue #302 may improve speakability, but is NOT QUALIFIED.
- Video Capture / Camera Operations candidate #294 owns technical source capture/audio quality where assigned, but does not currently establish narration-performance coaching.
- Video Editing & Post-Production 0.1.0 owns downstream audio editing/mix/repair, not live speaker training.

Relevant professional evidence:
- Rosenberg & Hirschberg (2009), DOI 10.1016/j.specom.2008.11.001: lexical/syntactic and acoustic/prosodic features are separable in charisma judgments.
- Al Jazeera Media Institute voice-over training explicitly includes pitch, articulation, delivery, pauses and microphone/studio technique.

## Project constraint

Goal is to preserve and improve the user's real voice, not default to synthetic voice replacement.

AI cleanup / post-processing may be used downstream, but it must not be confused with professional vocal-performance direction.

## Practical proof requirement

Any future capability must be evaluated on actual recorded takes from the same human narrator:
baseline -> direction -> new take(s) -> observable comparison -> downstream Post acceptance.

A written checklist alone cannot prove this capability.
