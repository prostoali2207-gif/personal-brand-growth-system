## Incident

- real business problems -> working systems;
- CRM / SaaS;
- automation and integrations;
- landing/product work;
- professional AI-agent engineering.

PBGS-IG-001 was structurally reframed so FleetDesk was "proof, not offer", but it still devoted most of its concrete proof and visual evidence to FleetDesk. When treated as the first brand-defining Reel, this creates a plausible first-contact classification error: the audience may infer that the professional category is FleetDesk/rental software rather than systems-building across multiple evidenced workstreams.

The incident is not repaired by changing the hook or inserting a list of services.

### External professional-practice evidence

Content strategy practice distinguishes strategy from individual editorial execution:
- Content Marketing Institute / Brain Traffic framing: content strategy determines the right content for the right people, time and reasons; editorial planning implements the strategy.
- Content Marketing Institute editorial-planning guidance treats the content mix as something derived from goals rather than a list of independently good assets.
- LinkedIn B2B Institute / Ehrenberg-Bass Category Entry Points work supports deliberate association between a brand and multiple relevant buying situations rather than allowing one narrow cue to define all mental availability.

These sources support the existence of a portfolio/message-strategy decision class. They do **not** prove that one specific launch sequence is universally correct.

## Applied responsibility

The capability owns only portfolio-level decisions such as:
- what professional/category perception initial exposure should establish;
- what role each evidence source/project plays in a content portfolio;
- whether a proposed asset is category-establishing, breadth-signaling, proof/depth, failure/repair, system-x-ray, decision/trade-off, or another strategy-approved role;
- first-contact / launch-batch composition and sequence logic where material;
- detection of **single-project category capture**;
- whether repeated narrow proof needs balancing with another evidence source;
- handing each asset a clear role/funnel/portfolio job before Content Architecture begins.

It does not own:
- final script/copy;
- single-asset semantic architecture;
- video capture/editing;
- publishing;
- KPI calculation or causal adjudication;
- unsupported service/experience claims.

## Required handoff to Content Architecture

When first-contact/sequence matters:

`PORTFOLIO_ROLE -> CONTENT_ARCHITECTURE`

Minimum fields:
- approved positioning;
- target audience;
- portfolio/first-contact objective;
- category/professional perception to establish;

- proposed asset role;

- public claim bounds.

If this handoff is absent, an individual case may be developed as a case asset but **must not be called the first/intro/launch brand-defining asset**.
