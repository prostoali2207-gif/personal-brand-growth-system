## FACT / EVIDENCE

1. Current positioning is practice-led / systems-builder: real operational problems -> working software, automations and professional AI-agent systems.
2. First-party naming/identity work repeatedly surfaced:
   - human control over automated systems;
   - current-version / working-copy / iteration;
   - practical application rather than theory;
   - technical workaround / operational reality;
   - evidence-first / as-found state.
3. The brand must not claim unsupported expert/guru authority.
4. Current AI/personal-brand market research shows increasing concern about generic, over-polished AI output; current guidance repeatedly emphasizes real work, judgment, proof, human voice and authentic process over generic AI packaging.
5. The current avatar render strongly communicates precision, systems and competence, but its cold metal + deep-black treatment pushes perceived formality/corporate severity beyond what the broader evidence requires.

## Hypothesis received

User observation: current avatar may feel too serious/strict.

Status: HYPOTHESIS / not treated as proof.

Professional review independently finds a real perception imbalance, but does not conclude that the brand should become playful or casual.

## Selected brand personality

### Core perception

**Precise, practical, curious, direct, human.**

Expanded:
**A capable systems-builder who works on real problems, shows the actual process, keeps judgment human, and is willing to expose iteration/failure without performing guru status or corporate perfection.**

### Personality axes

- precision: HIGH
- practicality: HIGH
- curiosity / experimentation: HIGH
- directness: HIGH
- human presence: MODERATE-HIGH
- warmth: MODERATE
- playfulness: LOW-MODERATE / optional
- formality: LOW-MODERATE
- prestige / luxury signaling: LOW
- guru / authority theater: VERY LOW
- visible iteration / work-in-progress: ALLOWED AND DESIRABLE when truthful

## Verbal translation

Prefer:
- concrete language;
- direct judgments with reasons;
- real project details;
- decisions, failures, repairs and trade-offs;
- natural human rhythm;
- occasional dry lightness when authentic.

Avoid:
- corporate consultancy voice;
- motivational AI-guru tone;
- forced slang;
- constant jokes;
- inflated certainty;
- “future of AI” grandiosity;
- polished generic AI copy.

## Visual translation

Preserve:
- precision;
- modular construction;
- strong silhouette;
- disciplined geometry;
- systems/interconnection logic.

Reduce:
- cold luxury/premium-tech feeling;
- deep-black abyss as the only emotional register;
- heavy metallic seriousness;
- cinematic intimidation;
- over-perfect corporate finish.

Introduce:
- slightly more human/tactile material character;
- softer or warmer environmental light/value relationship;
- clearer sense of active construction rather than monumental permanence;
- controlled imperfection/variation only where it does not weaken the mark.

Do NOT solve warmth by:
- mascots;
- childish colors;
- generic startup gradients;
- emojis;
- literal human faces inside the mark;
- random playful decoration.

## Market-intelligence limits

External sources are directional category evidence, not causal proof of what will perform for this account.

Current examples reviewed on 2026-09-11 emphasize:
- real work and judgment over generic AI packaging;
- authenticity/human connection as differentiation in AI-heavy personal branding;
- avoiding cold/generic AI identity while retaining technical credibility.

## Revisit triggers

Revisit personality only when:
- real audience/channel evidence materially contradicts the selected perception;
- positioning changes;
- repeated first-party communication evidence shows a different stable voice;
- a target audience decision requires a material adjustment.
