## User decision

Create one short general orientation Reel that tells a new viewer what Ali actually does:
- websites / landing pages;
- CRM;
- automation / integrations;
- custom internal systems.

The Reel may name several services because its primary job is **category orientation**, not proving one service.

## Portfolio role

### ORIENTATION-01 — "Чем я вообще занимаюсь"

Role:
**CATEGORY ORIENTATION / PROFILE ENTRY ASSET**

Job:
A new viewer should understand the commercial range in one pass without needing to infer it from several case videos.

Important:
This is an explicit exception to the normal one-primary-service-per-asset rule.

The asset must preserve service independence:
- a client may need only a landing;
- only CRM;
- only automation;
- only a custom system;
- or a combination where the actual business problem requires it.

No mandatory ladder or bundle is implied.

## Sequence

1. ORIENTATION-01 — category orientation.
2. PARTS-LANDING-01 — landing-specific proof/judgment.
3. BAYER-CRM-01 — CRM-specific proof/judgment.
4. FLEET-AUTO-01 — automation-specific proof/judgment.
5. CUSTOM-SYSTEM-01 — custom-system proof/judgment.
6. INTEGRATION-01 — integrated case when integration itself is the subject.
7. AI-METHOD-01 — secondary technical credibility.

## Pinning

Candidate recommendation:
ORIENTATION-01 is suitable as a profile/pinned orientation asset once approved and produced.

This is not authorization to publish or pin automatically.

## Guardrails

Do not:
- turn the Reel into a price/package pitch;
- imply every client needs the full stack;
- lead with AI;
- add unsupported outcomes/guarantees;
- make the service list sound like "I do literally everything";
- add a sales CTA while CTA remains unresolved.

## Hypothesis

A concise orientation asset followed by service-specific proof pieces will reduce first-contact category ambiguity better than forcing one case Reel to imply the entire service range.

This is not yet measured.
