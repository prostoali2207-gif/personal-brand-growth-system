## Business objective

Generate credible commercial demand for:
1. websites / landing pages;
2. CRM / lead-flow work;
3. business automation / integrations;
4. custom internal systems where the business need justifies them.

AI/professional-agent engineering is not the primary buyer category.

## Primary audience

**Owners, founders, managers and digitally responsible staff in small-to-mid-sized UAE businesses who need a practical digital result but are not required to be technical.**

Priority buyer profile:
- can recognize a business/customer/process problem;
- can approve or influence a website, CRM, automation or internal-system project;
- cares more about what will work in the business than the implementation vocabulary.

The audience must not be modeled as developers by default.

## Buyer situations — strategy hypotheses

These are targeting situations, not claimed prevalence statistics.

### S1 — "Нужен нормальный сайт / лендинг"
Business needs a credible digital front door for an offer, service or campaign.

Best-fit offer:
Website / Landing.

### S2 — "Заявка пришла — что дальше?"
Business receives enquiries through site/forms/WhatsApp/other channels but needs a clearer next-step flow.

Best-fit offer:
Lead Flow / CRM.

### S3 — "Слишком много ручной рутины"
Known repetitive actions or information handoffs are being done manually.

Best-fit offer:
Automation / Integrations.

### S4 — "Готовые инструменты уже не подходят"
Workflow needs specific states, roles, documents, calculations or business rules.

Best-fit offer:
Custom CRM / Internal System.

## Buyer-language rule

Primary content language at the concept level:
- site;
- landing page;
- enquiry / request;
- customer;
- WhatsApp;
- CRM;
- follow-up / next step;
- manual work;
- status;
- process;
- automation.

Technical language is secondary:
- backend;
- API;
- database;
- webhooks;
- agent qualification;
- evaluation infrastructure.

Use technical terms only when:
1. the buyer needs the distinction to make a decision; or
2. the term functions as proof after the business meaning is already clear.

## Audience hierarchy

### Primary
Non-technical or mixed-technicality UAE business decision-makers with a current digital/process need.

### Secondary
Marketing, sales, operations and digital staff who influence the buying decision or will use the resulting system.

### Tertiary / credibility audience
Developers, AI/automation builders and technical peers.

Their role:
- peer validation;
- collaboration;
- credibility;
- technical discussion.

They must not pull the main account back into an AI/developer-content category.

## Channel roles

### Instagram — primary discovery + trust + commercial relevance

Show:
- understandable business problem;
- visible artifact;
- what changed in the workflow/system;
- proof from real work;
- practical service relevance.

Do not require code literacy.

### LinkedIn — B2B credibility / deeper decision context

Use for:
- project breakdowns;
- why a business/UX/system decision was made;
- CRM/automation architecture translated into business language;
- deeper proof and constraints.

### TikTok — discovery test

Only adapt proven short-form mechanisms.
Do not create a separate technical-AI strategy.

### GitHub — proof substrate

Use as evidence, not as the buyer-facing explanation.

### AI/technical content

Allowed, but as a secondary series or deeper proof layer after the commercial category is clear.

## Content relevance test

Before an Instagram content brief is approved, answer:

**Could a normal business owner understand why this matters without knowing the implementation stack?**

If no:
- translate the business meaning;
- move technical depth later;
- or route the piece to a technical/secondary-audience series.

Do not delete legitimate technical content; classify it correctly.
