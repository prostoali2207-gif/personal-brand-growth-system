## FACT / OBSERVATION

1. The current avatar is not a direct copy of any observed reference.
2. Its exact geometry differs materially from the reviewed marks.
3. Its *visual mechanism* — dark circular/charcoal field + monochrome geometric interlocking system mark + dimensional/metallic treatment — overlaps with an established current AI/automation/tech visual vocabulary.
4. Elite Automation AI is the closest observed category-level mechanism overlap: layered/interlocking geometric construction on a charcoal field, used to communicate intelligent systems.
5. In personal-brand contexts, several observed practitioners use name-led or portrait-led identity systems, so a standalone abstract avatar can still differentiate at the profile level.
6. Current research does not establish trademark/legal uniqueness and is not a legal clearance.

### Root-cause classification

`CONCEPT/CONTRACT EDGE`, not implementation failure.

The layered-system concept is valid. The remaining problem is that its signature structural move is not yet strong enough to separate it from the broader family of dark geometric "intelligent systems" marks.

## Decision

**RENDER REVISE — DO NOT FREEZE AS FINAL YET.**

Do not restart from a different concept.

Preserve:
- layered/interlocking system logic;
- compact avatar silhouette;
- monochrome restraint;
- no literal AI symbols;
- independence from handle/initials.

Repair only:
1. introduce one unmistakable asymmetric signature joint / interruption / offset that cannot be reduced to a generic hexagon, S/Z, cube, folded ribbon or layered-E mechanism;
2. make that signature survive as a flat black/white silhouette at small size;
3. reduce reliance on metallic texture/lighting for identity;
4. retain 3D only as a secondary presentation treatment after the 2D mark is distinctive;
5. compare the repaired silhouette again specifically against Elite Automation / Autoblocks-like geometric-system language before final freeze.

## Constraints

- no new concept discovery;
- no AI brain / robot / circuit / neon trope;
- no handle-derived initials;
- no post-hoc story to justify arbitrary form;
- no claim of legal/trademark clearance.

## Revisit trigger

Run one repaired render through the same narrow gate. If the signature move survives small-scale silhouette inspection and materially separates the mark from the observed category mechanisms, freeze the avatar as final.
