## Professional diagnosis

1. Big object -> a supposed move/house relocation.
2. Caring wrapping -> imaginary car jealousy.
3. Many parts -> fictional IKEA-style "assemble Kia" punchline.
These are transplantable to arbitrary parts/shipment footage. Weak surprise, no observed human irony, no genuinely specific connection between words and edit. Repeating these amounts to disconnected comedic narration, not purposeful situational humor.
