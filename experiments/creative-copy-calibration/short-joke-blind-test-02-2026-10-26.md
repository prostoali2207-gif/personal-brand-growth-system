## Research question
The first three jokes all failed (creator direct score 0/3). Test whether broadening conceptual domain, allowing uncomfortable social satire and avoiding gentle office/tech generic incongruity yields even one joke the creator actually finds funny. This is a within-one-person qualitative exploration, not statistical inference.

## Locked texts; copy exactly, without explanation, ranking or model favorite

A:
Парикмахер однажды подстриг меня криво. Уже четвёртый год спрашивает: «Как обычно?»

B:
Сосед купил замок за двадцать тысяч. Вор вскрыл его и оставил отзыв: «Не стоит своих денег».

C:
На открытии приюта для бездомных всех бездомных попросили уйти. Приехали фотографы.

## Separation of mechanisms (do not reveal until after rating)
- A: error becomes a persistent ritual; the person receiving a service has been trapped by the normalizing question, realistic everyday specificity. Risk: might feel obvious on second thought.
- B: thief as product reviewer after evaluating expensive security; switch to unlikely evaluator / consumer culture absurdity. Risk: the thief would have little reason to leave a review, so it relies on a specific fictional exaggeration.
- C: cynical institutional image-management physically contradicts stated humanitarian purpose; sharp social satire with an actual harm of displaced homeless people represented as a **clearly hypothetical sketch**, not a real named incident. Risk: too plausible/bleak to register as comedy.
- Do not create stories that imply real people or organizations actually did these things.
- Different contexts and targets: barbershop daily speech, home security + buyer review, public image management. No corporate HR/website clichés from test 01.

## Evaluation
- Display A/B/C alone. Ask which is genuinely funny or "none." Optional one-sentence reason especially if none.
- Do not coach creator, give mechanism names, praise variants or smuggle favorite into prompt before vote.
- Record creator assessment as first-party evidence after response, preserving original locked variants.
- Neither human taste nor lint indicates universal joke quality; two consecutive failed short tests should trigger hypothesis revision or Russian-native reference review, not unlimited one-liner cycling.

## Diagnosis (professional judgment / hypothesis only)
- A: safe, mundane annoyance with a barber, negligible stakes, familiar setup/payoff. Nothing morbid, transgressive or forceful.
- B: playful cartoon thief/product reviewer, no real consequence or sting. Invented absurdity without darker recognition.
- C: cynical publicity/charity optics but telegraphs the hypocrisy, does not deliver a sharper secondary reframe. Poverty/homelessness is present as a dramatic backdrop, not an earned black-humor mechanism.
- Portfolio problem: topic variety increased, *emotional and transgressive intensity did not*. We changed skin rather than core creative stance; "clever" inversion repeatedly mistaken for a laugh.
- Do not "correct" this by mass profanity, explicit sex, gratuitous cruelty or jokes at the expense of real identifiable victims; none was requested.

## Revised next step
1. Stop new generic A/B/C joke generation for this round. After **two 0/3 trials**, perform **targeted benchmark calibration for mordant/black humor in Russian** (e.g. absurdist deadpan and cynical social satire) using real credited examples; distinguish shocking imagery from genuine comic turn.
2. Ask the creator to select among 2–3 *recognizable dark-humor directions* using actual attributed source examples or very short new clearly fictional calibration lines; record a feedback signal about the comedic register. Do not assume dark=sexual.
3. Then lock new short joke trial (#03) in 3 genuinely dissimilar dark mechanisms, no tutorial or hints before judgment; preserve original variants. Optional early micro-test with original fictional examples, but never claim they are independently excellent.
4. Preserve separate production gate: personal-business content requires relevance, verified project reality and channel/audience suitability; a black-humor preference in a joke workshop does not approve publishing morbid jokes about real clients.
