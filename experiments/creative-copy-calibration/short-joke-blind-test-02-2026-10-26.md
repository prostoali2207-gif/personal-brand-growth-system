## Research question
The first three jokes all failed (creator direct score 0/3). Test whether broadening conceptual domain, allowing uncomfortable social satire and avoiding gentle office/tech generic incongruity yields even one joke the creator actually finds funny. This is a within-one-person qualitative exploration, not statistical inference.

A:

Сосед купил замок за двадцать тысяч. Вор вскрыл его и оставил отзыв: «Не стоит своих денег».

На открытии приюта для бездомных всех бездомных попросили уйти. Приехали фотографы.

## Separation of mechanisms (do not reveal until after rating)
- A: error becomes a persistent ritual; the person receiving a service has been trapped by the normalizing question, realistic everyday specificity. Risk: might feel obvious on second thought.
- B: thief as product reviewer after evaluating expensive security; switch to unlikely evaluator / consumer culture absurdity. Risk: the thief would have little reason to leave a review, so it relies on a specific fictional exaggeration.
- C: cynical institutional image-management physically contradicts stated humanitarian purpose; sharp social satire with an actual harm of displaced homeless people represented as a **clearly hypothetical sketch**, not a real named incident. Risk: too plausible/bleak to register as comedy.
- Do not create stories that imply real people or organizations actually did these things.
- Different contexts and targets: barbershop daily speech, home security + buyer review, public image management. No corporate HR/website clichés from test 01.

## Evaluation
- Display A/B/C alone. Ask which is genuinely funny or "none." Optional one-sentence reason especially if none.
- Do not coach creator, give mechanism names, praise variants or smuggle favorite into prompt before vote.
- Record creator assessment as first-party evidence after response, preserving original locked variants.
- Neither human taste nor lint indicates universal joke quality; two consecutive failed short tests should trigger hypothesis revision or Russian-native reference review, not unlimited one-liner cycling.
