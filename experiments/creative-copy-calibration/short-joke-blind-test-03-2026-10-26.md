- C: dystopian civic triumph: new mortuary falsely equated with efficiency in medical queues, dark institutional reframing. Fictional and unnamed municipality. Risk: echoes prior test 02 institutional irony, despite darker logic.
- Not a progression from soft to explicit/sexual. All non-vulgar and short. Broadly three distinct emotional targets: existential, workplace cruelty, public-health policy satire.
