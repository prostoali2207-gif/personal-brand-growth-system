## Evidence limitations and next research operation
- Search provided real permalinks; Instagram exposed captions and post metadata, not dependable primary video streams here.
- Do NOT conflate a caption with a voice-over transcript, or a link with inspected performance.
- First obtain directly watchable original artifacts with accessible speech/transcription and visuals from original Instagram/YouTube/TikTok creator uploads; inspect more than one artifact per creator where acceptance relies on recurrent excellence.
- Avoid using appearance of women or prohibited music in **user-facing produced brand content**. Reference evidence can be mechanically analyzed for writing without reproducing soundtrack/visuals in output.
- Broaden the voice/craft pool beyond technology and service-business marketing: concise mini-documentary, observational storytelling, product demonstrations and natural spoken Russian; explain transferability and non-transferable stylistic elements.
- Prefer 3–5 complementary actually-inspected references; no forced numeric quota. Benchmark status remains RESEARCH_REQUIRED until video and language can be inspected.

## Experiment design (not executed)
Input: at least two independent real clips/rough cuts with a shot list + verified facts. Prefer (A) service-business site/CRM demonstration, (B) physical-process or product-handling sequence. Keep source video unchanged between arms.
Arms:
- A: model ordinary short prompt, no elite benchmark.
- B: same model with exact qualified Creative/Architecture contracts and inspected benchmark mechanisms.
- C: optionally same conditions in another model (Claude vs ChatGPT) only if sufficient resources.
Procedure: freeze briefs and footage; anonymize output; randomize ordering; independent human comparative review (ideally practitioner plus target-audience member), not sole model self-grading; document edits and reasons; compare previously unseen tasks, not benchmark examples.
Judgment: natural spoken language, specific thought, authentic evidence, unique mechanism, hook-to-payoff alignment, visual fit, audience relevance, absence of filler/cliches, no invented facts. Report each dimension separately and whether a human would release with no material rewrites.
Pass logic: no claimed winner until evaluated; a practical quality gain must recur across different tasks; failures route to the responsible skill/core/eval layer, not more emphatic generic prompts.
Stopping rule: if B does not consistently outperform A on blind evaluations, don't assert calibration works; identify missing video access, reference validity, taste evaluation, or script-generation issue.
Reversibility: this is a draft research/experiment record only; no videos republished, no external assets cloned, no automated posting.
