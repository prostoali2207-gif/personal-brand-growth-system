## Controls, limitations, evaluation plan
- This arm assignment is **hidden from the first user-facing video comparison**. The GitHub research log preserves the assignment for later audit.
- Both texts drafted by the same assistant in a single task context. Therefore **NOT** an independent randomized model-condition comparison. No expert judgement has been collected.
- Captions differ in exact words; footage, display style, and durations are identical. This is a *text preference / script-craft pilot* with visual context, not a test of paid/organic platform metrics or audio naturalness.
- Need viewer ranking and preferably blinded independent professional validation for a genuine quality claim.
- Rating dimensions (distinct, do not collapse prematurely): attention/hook; clear specific meaning; fit to actual shots; natural Russian speech; absence of boilerplate; no fictional claim; whether human would publish without rewrite.
- Single case cannot establish generalization across categories, formats and language.
- **PASS state must not be recorded until** human blind feedback is observed and a representative set of additional unseen clips is tested with correct isolation.
- User may reject BOTH variants; preserve rejection as a substantive quality-signal rather than compelling a false winner.
