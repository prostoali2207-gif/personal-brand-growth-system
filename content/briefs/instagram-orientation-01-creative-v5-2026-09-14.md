## Why this is stronger

- starts with proof rather than self-description;
- viewer can immediately map words to real artifacts;
- contains one clear professional abstraction instead of a flat service list;
- no internal constraint/disclaimer leaks into copy;
- no guru/expert positioning claim;
- no result claim;
- no CTA;
- spoken syntax is intentionally compact and slightly uneven rather than brochure-polished;
- closure stays inside ongoing practitioner work instead of summarizing a lesson.

## Claim safety

- `с которыми я сейчас работаю` does not claim sole authorship;
- service range is current user-confirmed commercial direction;
- no revenue, conversion, time-saved, user-count, guarantee or unsupported effectiveness claim.
