## COMMUNICATION JOB

Answer one question for a new viewer:

**"Чем этот человек вообще занимается и с какими задачами к нему можно прийти?"**

The Reel does not need to prove every service. Proof comes in subsequent case content.

## ATTENTION CONTRACT

Opening should acknowledge the orientation need directly and casually.

Preferred function:
**"Чтобы сразу было понятно, чем я вообще занимаюсь..."**

Avoid:
- grand positioning statement;
- "я digital-эксперт";
- "я создаю инновационные решения";
- technical-stack opening;
- AI-first framing.

## INFORMATION ORDER

### B1 — ORIENTATION
One short line establishing why the list is being given.

### B2 — SERVICE RANGE
Name the current buyer-facing modules in ordinary language:
- sites / landing pages;
- CRM;
- automation / integrations;
- custom internal systems.

### B3 — INDEPENDENCE
Make clear these are not a mandatory bundle:
sometimes one task is enough; sometimes several need to work together.

### B4 — CONTENT PROMISE
Say what the account will actually show:
real projects, decisions, failures/repairs, how the systems are built/work.

No generic "полезный контент" promise.

### B5 — STOP
No sales CTA until approved upstream.

## PACING INTENT

Target:
roughly 20–30 seconds spoken, depending on delivery.

- 0–15%: orientation line;
- 15–60%: service range;
- 60–80%: standalone/composable clarification;
- 80–100%: real-work content promise.

## VISUAL COMMUNICATION

Preferred:
quick real project glimpses matching each named category where available:
- landing/site screen;
- CRM screen;
- FleetDesk/automation state;
- internal-system screen.

Do not fake a visual for a service.

This Reel may be mostly talking head / voice-over with real screens; exact capture belongs downstream.

## MUST PRESERVE

- modular service model;
- ordinary buyer language;
- no AI as primary category;
- no performance claims;
- no pricing/package/guarantee;
- no CTA;
- real-work promise only.

## MAY CHOOSE

- exact spoken register;
- whether to say "сайт и лендинг" separately or together;
- "автоматизация" vs "убрать ручную рутину" phrasing;
- amount of dry humor;
- final short line.
