## COMMUNICATION JOB

Answer one question for a new viewer:

**Чем Али вообще занимается?**

## PROFESSIONAL DECISION

Do not open with an abstract service card while verified project evidence is available.

Use three real project surfaces first, then generalize the range of work from them.

Why:
- proof arrives before self-description;
- the viewer sees that the categories come from actual work;
- the orientation becomes specific to this portfolio instead of interchangeable agency copy;
- evidence-first execution supports creator-practitioner mode.

## CONSTRAINT SURFACE MAP

| Constraint | Lock | Surface | Execution |
|---|---|---|---|
| service categories are modular | HARD | INTERNAL_ONLY | do not imply forced sequence |
| AI is not the buyer-facing front door | HARD | INTERNAL_ONLY | do not lead with AI |
| no unsupported results / guarantees | HARD | INTERNAL_ONLY | omit them |
| no CTA approved | HARD | INTERNAL_ONLY | stop without CTA |
| real project evidence | COMMUNICATION | PUBLIC_MESSAGE_REQUIRED | show/name verified projects |
| range: sites/landings, CRM, automation/integrations, internal systems | COMMUNICATION | PUBLIC_MESSAGE_REQUIRED | infer/generalize after proof |
| account shows real work and decisions | COMMUNICATION | PUBLIC_MESSAGE_REQUIRED | compact close |

## ATTENTION CONTRACT

`real evidence -> professional abstraction -> ongoing work promise -> stop`

Not:
`service list -> explanation -> disclaimer -> summary`.

## INFORMATION ORDER

### B1 — EVIDENCE TRIAD
Fast introduction of three real projects:
- Auto-parts landing;
- BayerCRM;
- FleetDesk.

Do not explain each project yet.

### B2 — PROFESSIONAL ABSTRACTION
Name the common work pattern in ordinary language:
take a concrete business task and build the appropriate digital solution around it.

Then name the current range:
- websites / landing pages;
- CRM;
- automation / integrations;
- internal systems.

### B3 — ACCOUNT PROMISE
Show the work itself:
- decisions;
- failures/breakage;
- revisions;
- reasons behind changes.

### B4 — STOP
No CTA. No lesson recap.

## PACING

Target: ~15–22 seconds depending on natural delivery.

## VISUAL PROOF

Open on real project surfaces, not logos alone:
- landing screen;
- BayerCRM working screen;
- FleetDesk working screen.

Use quick but readable cuts. No arrows/funnel/dependency graphics.

## MUST PRESERVE SILENTLY

- no service dependency implication;
- no AI-first framing;
- no unsupported outcomes;
- no CTA;
- no sole-authorship implication beyond evidenced/user-confirmed role.
