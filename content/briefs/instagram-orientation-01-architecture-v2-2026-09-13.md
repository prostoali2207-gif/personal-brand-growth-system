## COMMUNICATION JOB

Answer one question for a new viewer:

**Чем Али вообще занимается?**

Nothing else needs to be explained in this asset.

## ATTENTION CONTRACT

Low-distance orientation.
The opening should feel like a direct answer, not a manifesto or advertisement.

Function:
`quick context -> service range -> what the account will show -> stop`

## INFORMATION ORDER

### B1 — QUICK CONTEXT
One short spoken line: what this account/person does.

### B2 — SERVICE RANGE
Name the current buyer-facing range in ordinary language:
- sites / landing pages;
- CRM;
- automation / integrations;
- internal business systems.

Do not explain relationships between categories.
Do not defend the list.
Do not explain what the user does NOT need.

### B3 — REAL-WORK PROMISE
Briefly state that the account will show real projects/work from inside:
- what is being built;
- what breaks;
- what changes;
- why decisions are made.

### B4 — STOP
No CTA.

## PACING

Shorter than v1.
Target roughly 12–20 seconds depending on natural delivery.

Priority:
- context: very short;
- service range: brisk;
- real-work promise: one compact closing thought.

## VISUAL PROOF

Use real project glimpses corresponding to the named categories where available.

Visuals may show breadth, but must not use arrows/funnel/dependency choreography.

## MUST PRESERVE SILENTLY

- service independence;
- AI secondary role;
- evidence/claim safety;
- no CTA.

These are NOT public message blocks.
