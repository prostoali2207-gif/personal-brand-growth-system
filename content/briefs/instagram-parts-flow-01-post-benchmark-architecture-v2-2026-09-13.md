## Revision diagnosis

Previous architecture was truthful, but it was created before the elite niche benchmark.

Benchmark review exposed a structural weakness:
- the old attention contract was primarily a polite process question;
- it did not force visible professional judgment;
- the project risked becoming an explanatory walkthrough instead of evidence of how Ali thinks.

This is a STRUCTURAL REVISION, not a strategy change.

Preserved upstream locks:
- ordinary non-technical business buyer;
- Website/Landing is the front-door service;
- CRM/integration is secondary depth;
- real landing -> BayerCRM proof;
- no AI/backend lead;
- no performance/revenue claims;
- no invented CTA.

## Structural divergence

### OPTION A — CLIENT/BUSINESS BOUNDARY CONTRAST

Attention job:
make the viewer notice that the customer's action ending does not mean the business workflow ends.

Opening function:
for customer: submit = end of action / for business: submit = start of work

Proof order:
1. landing form;
2. Submit;
3. BayerCRM destination;
4. short judgment about designing beyond the visible page.

Strength:
very buyer-readable.

Weakness:
risk of sounding like a generic systems principle if authorial judgment stays too soft.

### OPTION B — LIVE FORM DECISION / CRITIQUE

Attention job:
show one specific design decision before explaining the system.

Opening function:
why is this not just one generic 'tell us what you need' field?

Proof order:
1. real form on screen;
2. point to vehicle / multiple parts / photos;
3. explain the request structure;
4. submit;
5. reveal corresponding CRM request;
6. state the professional judgment: a landing form should fit the real business request when the workflow requires it.

Strength:
- makes expertise observable;
- project-specific;
- naturally connects web work to CRM depth;
- closest to elite-benchmark pattern: artifact -> judgment -> proof -> commercial implication.

Weakness:
must not overclaim that every business needs structured forms.

### OPTION C — REAL DEMO COLD OPEN

Attention job:
let proof carry the opening.

Opening function:
watch one real request travel from landing to CRM

Proof order:
1. rapid real input;
2. Submit;
3. switch to CRM;
4. reveal same structured request;
5. one short judgment at end.

Strength:
highest proof density; low explanatory overhead.

Weakness:
without a strong spoken judgment, may become a product demo rather than personal-brand content.

## Convergence

SELECT OPTION B — LIVE FORM DECISION / CRITIQUE.

Reason:
it repairs the exact weakness found by the benchmark: the viewer sees not only what the system does, but what professional decision Ali is evaluating and why.

It also keeps the service front door legible:
the visible object is still a landing/form, while CRM appears as deeper capability.

Option C remains a production fallback if the exact source capture is visually strong enough to carry the opening.

## COMMUNICATION_JOB

Make a normal business owner infer:

'This person does not only make a page look finished; he thinks about what information the business actually needs from the customer and what happens to that request next.'

Do not say that sentence directly.

## ATTENTION_CONTRACT

Immediate viewer question:

Why isn't this request form just one generic message field?

Truthful payoff owed:
because this real workflow needs structured vehicle/part/photo information, and the submitted request continues into BayerCRM.

The opening must be phrased as a decision/question, not a universal rule.

Allowed functional forms:
- 'Можно было сделать здесь одно поле...'
- 'Вот почему здесь не одно поле...'
- 'Я бы не начинал эту форму с Опишите, что вам нужно...'

Creator chooses exact wording.

Forbidden:
- 'обычные сайты теряют заявки';
- 'так конверсия выше';
- 'так быстрее';
- 'так правильно для любого бизнеса';
unless separately evidenced.

## INFORMATION_ORDER

### B1 — VISIBLE DECISION
Job:
show the real form immediately and identify the design choice being discussed.

Information:
the request is structured around the actual request context, not one undifferentiated text message.

Proof:
real landing form.

### B2 — WHY THE STRUCTURE EXISTS
Job:
make the business logic understandable.

Information:
this implemented flow supports:
- vehicle context;
- multiple parts per vehicle;
- photos.

Proof:
real fields / interaction.

Boundary:
do not generalize this structure to every landing page.

### B3 — WHAT HAPPENS AFTER SUBMIT
Job:
reveal depth.

Information:
the structured request proceeds into BayerCRM for manager workflow.

Proof:
corresponding CRM request/destination.

This is the main payoff.

### B4 — PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT
Job:
make Ali's thinking visible.

Required meaning:
when the business process requires it, the landing/form should be designed around the real request and its next step, not treated as an isolated visual page.

Creator owns wording.

### B5 — COMMERCIAL CONTINUITY
Job:
allow the viewer to infer service breadth.

Meaning:
website/landing first; CRM/integration can continue the work where needed.

Do not list all services.

## PROOF_PLAN

Required:
1. real landing form;
2. fields demonstrating request structure;
3. submit state;
4. corresponding BayerCRM destination.

No code is required for primary proof.

Source QC remains:
FUNCTIONAL_PROOF_PASS / VISUAL_SOURCE_INCOMPLETE.

Therefore:
creative can proceed;
production cannot claim SOURCE_READY until the matching visual transition is captured.
