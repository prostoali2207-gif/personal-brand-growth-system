# Current commercial intent

record_id: PBGS-SOT-COMMERCIAL-001
scope: personal professional brand / client acquisition
status: CURRENT / USER-CONFIRMED
verified_at: 2026-09-13
source_type: explicit user direction

## Primary commercial objective

Use the personal brand to attract **ordinary business clients who need practical digital work**, not an audience that must understand AI or software engineering.

Current service direction includes:
- websites / landing pages;
- CRM systems;
- business-process automation;
- integrations and related business systems.

This is a commercial direction, not a finalized productized-service catalog.

## Service independence — confirmed 2026-09-13

The current service directions are **independent/composable**, not a mandatory sequence.

A client may legitimately need:
- only a website / landing page;
- only CRM work;
- only automation / integration;
- only a custom internal system;
- or a combination when the actual problem requires it.

Do not infer that a landing should connect to CRM, or that CRM should lead to automation, merely because those capabilities can be combined.

Breadth in public content should normally emerge across the portfolio rather than by forcing several service categories into every asset.

## Buyer-language rule

The intended buyer may be non-technical.

They should not need to understand terms such as:
- backend;
- agent qualification;
- model/evaluation infrastructure;
- implementation architecture;

unless a specific piece of content genuinely requires that detail and translates it into buyer-relevant meaning.

## AI role

AI / professional-agent engineering is:
- a real capability/evidence layer;
- an implementation method where useful;
- a possible differentiator in selected contexts.

It is **not** currently the primary thing the general client must buy, understand, or identify the brand with.

Do not turn the account into:
- an AI-news account;
- a technical AI education account;
- an agent-engineering account for developers;
- a portfolio whose main category is AI expertise.

## Public communication implication

Technical evidence should answer buyer-relevant questions such as:
- what business problem is being solved;
- what the customer/user can now do;
- what process becomes connected or automated;
- why a landing/site/CRM/system is structured this way;
- what was broken and what was fixed.

Technical implementation may be shown as proof, but it must not become the communication job by default.

## Unresolved commercial work

Not yet fixed by this record:
- exact service packages;
- pricing;
- scope boundaries;
- ideal first offer;
- guarantees;
- delivery model;
- exact CTA / lead path.

Those require a separate commercial/offer decision and must not be invented by Content or Strategy.

## Supersession rule

Any prior strategy/content decision that treated applied AI / professional-agent engineering as a primary first-contact category must be revalidated against this record before further production.
