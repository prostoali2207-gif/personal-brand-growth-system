## Verified facts / locked strategy

Positioning:
- **Real business problems -> working systems.**

Primary audience:
- owner-operators, operations leaders and digitally responsible managers in small-to-mid-sized service businesses with real workflow/system friction;
- UAE-first market wedge;
- automotive/mobility is the strongest current evidence-backed wedge, not a permanent category boundary.

Secondary audience:
- applied-AI, automation, SaaS/CRM and professional-agent builders.

Channel roles:
- Instagram: primary discovery + trust;
- LinkedIn: B2B credibility + depth;
- TikTok: controlled discovery experiment;
- Telegram: deferred retention/depth;
- YouTube: deferred;
- GitHub: proof layer, not reach channel.

Strategic mechanism hypothesis:
- start from recognizable operational pain;
- reveal the system/workflow decision;
- show concrete implementation/evidence;
- expose failures/repairs/trade-offs;
- create credibility from real work rather than generic AI commentary.

## Must preserve

- evidence-first content source;
- separation of FACT / CORE JUDGMENT / HYPOTHESIS;
- no unsupported personal authorship, revenue, savings, client-result or performance claims;
- no generic AI-news editorial pivot;
- no assumption that one asset is copied identically across channels;
- candidate upstream cores remain labeled NOT QUALIFIED.

## Content Architecture authority

Owns:
- attention contract / opening job;
- semantic and narrative structure;
- proof architecture;
- macro pacing and information density;
- visual communication requirements;
- structural placement of an already-approved CTA/offer;
- creator-ready must_preserve / bounded / may_choose / must_escalate constraints.

Does not own:
- audience / funnel / KPI / experiment decision;
- final script, caption, title, thumbnail or CTA wording;
- exact editing / transitions / grading / audio;
- publishing;
- measurement adjudication.

## Requested output

Create:
1. a small number of repeatable content structures, not generic topical pillars;
2. a proof architecture for each structure;
3. channel-specific structural adaptation for Instagram and LinkedIn;
4. a first batch of evidence-backed content opportunities routed from the four verified projects;
5. constraints ready for downstream Social Content Creative.

Do not set cadence or volume merely to fill a calendar.
