## Mechanism exemplars: 10 case references

1. **Steven Wright, skylight and upstairs residents**.
   - Source, credited comedian and mechanism explained: https://www.toastmasters.org/magazine/magazine-issues/2026/june/the-elements-of-comedic-surprise
   - Observable structure: an innocent domestic home-improvement statement changes meaning after the listener learns there is an upstairs apartment.
   - Transferable: revise a taken-for-granted physical/spatial assumption. Do not just mention an implausible fact and call it absurd.

2. **Mitch Hedberg, escalator becomes stairs**.
   - Source: https://www.american.edu/kogod/students/communication/upload/mitch-hedberg-on-marketing.pdf
   - Observable: reframes a machine failure as another ordinary function that continues to work, mocking bureaucratic failure language.
   - Transferable: reveal ordinary system labels contradict the thing directly observable.

3. **Mitch Hedberg, paper receipt for a doughnut**.
   - Source: same American University teaching compilation, with references to retail transactions.
   - Observable: subjects a mundane receipt to an implausibly suspicious forensic situation, revealing how ritual procedures outgrow real need.
   - Transferable: notice real overdocumentation and build situational overcommitment, not merely 'paperwork is stupid'.
   - Same SOURCE dependency as #2, not independent confirmation.

4. **Norm Macdonald, moth at podiatrist**.
   - Source comedian's firsthand account: https://lasvegasmagazine.com/interviews/qa/2016/feb/05/norm-macdonald-hard-rock-hotel-comedy/
   - Mechanism: an extremely long dramatic tangent deliberately amplifies the disproportion with a trivial literal payoff, in front of reactive host/audience; comic actor's performance carries substance, not words alone.
   - Transferability LIMIT: the recorded bit is several minutes, **do not emulate with a simple 10-second joke**; demonstrates pacing/commitment and comic identity. He says this long improvised variation sprang from a short preexisting joke.

5. **Anthony Jeselnik, iterative third-thought misdirection**.
   - Source, first-person interview: https://www.fastcompany.com/3052383/too-far-or-not-far-enough-how-anthony-jeselnik-writes-and-tests-his-non-pc-standup-act
   - Method: make expectation list; reject most obvious payoff and easily predicted second payoff; seek a surprising third interpretation. He discards material that does not elicit a strong live-audience reaction.
   - Transferable: deliberate search for non-obvious outcome; user wants **non-vulgar default**, not Jeselnik's signature violent/taboo subject matter.

6. **George Carlin, public-policy euphemism critique**.
   - Source with actual excerpt and explanatory context: https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/a-23-a-2002-02-01-27-1-83112787/118011.html
   - Mechanism: exposes the hypocrisy by contrasting organizations' inflated or evasive official language with the concrete act behind it.
   - Transferable: interrogate the exact public or business phrase rather than deliver vague 'corporations bad' cynicism; stage bit is longer than a short joke.

7. **James Acaster, tea-bag analogy about Brexit**.
   - Source interview: https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2018/jul/01/james-acaster-interview-netflix-series-repertoire-latitude
   - Mechanism: a genuinely specific extended comparison to analyze a real political choice from unexpected ordinary domestic mechanics; comic style also depends on performed persona and audience.
   - Transferability LIMIT: resembles the formulaic comparisons rejected by creator if copied as a 'random X like Y'. Adopt only the exact causal parallel and grounded observation, **not the comparison syntax by default**.

8. **Adam Rowe, being fired from a jobcentre**.
   - Source compiled actual 2018 Joke of Fringe winner: https://www.timeout.com/edinburgh/edinburgh-fringe-best-jokes
   - Mechanism: institutional/job paradox: someone dismissed from a workplace still has to visit the same place in a different role. Distinctly situational, no loose random noun pun.
   - Award proves selection in a British joke competition, not creator-specific humor quality.

9. **Ian Smith, bath-bomb relaxation vs carrying product in rain**.
   - Source: 2025 voted joke shortlist https://www.comedy.co.uk/awards/directory/dave-funniest-joke-of-the-fringe-award/2025/
   - Mechanism: literal physical consequence of the described product undermines its advertised emotional promise, rather than random simile.
   - The specific experience is often the key; avoid copying "product X is relaxing unless..." phrase. Award source has a small local judging sample, not representative poll.

10. **Li Jin Hao, slogan interpreted as a list of priorities**.
    - 2026 critic-curated Fringe joke, reported by The Guardian: https://www.theguardian.com/stage/2026/aug/20/magicians-and-margarine-10-of-the-funniest-jokes-from-the-edinburgh-fringe-2026
    - Mechanism: treats an earnest moral declaration's ordering as unintended literal admissions. Punch derives logically from speaker's exact wording.
    - Transferable: exact unintended implications of stock slogans; do not mirror its ideological target or sentence verbatim.

## Bounded actionable synthesis / professional judgments

- Real top-tier performances use different mechanisms: physical causal paradox, institutional hypocrisy, linguistic implication, commitment to intentionally exaggerated dramatic setup, surprise expectation inversion, and precise lived observation.
- Elite benchmarking should take **mechanisms** not signature phrases: for user we test the third thought and real-specific institutional contradictions, and avoid punchline inflation.
- Risk: novelty is not equal to funny; the 2026 AI output could still miss human taste. A blind A/B/C audience test is needed.
- Joke tests with no external footage may use clearly fictional hypothetical setups. They should not make statements about real user, real company, or real customer.
- Corpus should expand to stronger native Russian-comedy samples in subsequent cycles **only after** direct current short-joke feedback demonstrates a gap. Do not turn reference research into endless collection.
