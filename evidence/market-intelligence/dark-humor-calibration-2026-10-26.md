## Source 1: Russian-language absurd deadpan
- Literature study: https://d-harms.ru/library/kobrinskiy-daniil-harms9.html, A.A. Kobrinsky on Daniil Kharms' `Вываливающиеся старухи` from `Случаи`.
- Context interpretation: a chain of terrible events is narrated without expected empathy/pathos; final narrator's radically ordinary change of interest reframes the prior catastrophic absurdity.
- Mechanism: (1) severe but clearly *fictional* transgression, (2) deadpan narrator, (3) radically mismatched scale of attention. The abrupt change in perspective, not the mention of injury itself, creates the effect.
- Not to transfer: actual old-person injury or the original plot as a recycled gag. Preserve original work attribution. A historical dark absurdist is not a validated model for the creator's audience.

## Source 2: Active working Russian comedy contextual limits
- https://gostandup.ru/articles/intervyu_s_rezidentami_standup_club_1 (comedians at Stand-Up Club #1 discuss dark comedy and audience expectation; indicate dark jokes were set aside for dark-night performances and audience context).
- https://gostandup.ru/articles/zametki_s_chernogo_stendapa_v_standup_club_1 (event report distinguished that some performers rely heavily on sex/porn gags, while some audiences expected bleak, death- or violence-related cynicism).
- Mechanism to transfer: "black comedy" is not a single semantic theme, is not a synonym for vulgar sexual jokes, and requires expectation calibration. These reports are anecdotal and >10 years old, not evidence of audience prevalence.

## Source 3: Professional dark punchline craft
- Comedian interview: https://www.ocweekly.com/anthony-jeselnik-will-offend-you-and-youll-like-it-6601993/
- Another interview: https://www.fastcompany.com/1682647/anthony-jeselnik-on-how-to-be-funny-by-being-mean
- Jeselnik explains exploring several possible continuations of a setup, hiding the punchline, and differentiating *merely offensive words* from a genuinely surprising twist.
- Mechanism to transfer: construct the initially plausible meaning -> hide the darker literal reinterpretation -> land on a specific unexpected conclusion. A black word is not a payoff.
- Limitation: his stage persona and culturally sensitive material are neither mandatory nor suited automatically to this brand.

## Source 4: Evaluated psychological theory with limitations
- https://doi.org/10.1177/1088868320961909 Warren, Barsky, McGraw integrative review: multiple mechanisms rather than one universal joke formula; violation and appraisal matter, and surprise alone not sufficient.
- https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6593112/ emphasizes audience social/psychological distance and context; too harmless OR too extreme can fail.
- Experimental/theoretical models predict neither one person's specific laughter nor content safety acceptability. Do not use the models to declare new jokes funny.

## Revision protocol for test #03
1. First calibrate *register*, separate axes: (a) cynical institutional hypocrisy; (b) morbid deadpan absurd; (c) cold unexpected reversal with taboo-ish premise. Do not conflate grimness with explicit sex, gore, real victim mockery or targeted hate.
2. Unlike #01 and #02, test three **independently distinguishable emotional/comic mechanisms** rather than simply changing nouns, workplace, shop or actor.
3. Use self-contained fictional sketches with clear cues, avoid fake claims about real people. Make the dark conceptual turn the punchline. Allow understatement.
4. A/B/C should each be short enough to say naturally, no generic anticipation or explained moral. No user-facing "this one's strongest".
5. Choose rating metric simply: A/B/C/none and optional user remark "слишком мягко / слишком мрачно / банально / непонятно". Do not turn user into a questionnaire.
6. If #03 also fails, do not keep guessing: get direct first-party **actual jokes or sketches they themselves find funny**, attributed/linked, before further training iteration.
7. Human / creator result is the ONLY pass criterion. Scripts from source research are mechanisms to reason about, not jokes to copy.
