User requested 3–5 current videos per strong creator, prioritizing views, audience reaction where observable, commercial relevance, creative quality and transferability to Ali's offer.

"Most viewed" is not enough. A tool-news video can attract designers/developers rather than business buyers.

Rank on two separate dimensions:
1. distribution signal — views / relative outlier / likes / comments where available;
2. commercial-transfer signal — usefulness for buyer-facing service content.

Public sources do not expose comment sentiment consistently. Do not treat comment count as positive sentiment and do not fabricate dislike/sentiment data. Metrics are snapshots.

# Cross-sample findings

1. High views and high commercial transfer are not the same.
2. Strong pieces rarely open with "what I do."
3. Professional judgment is the differentiator: what the creator rejects, notices, changes and prioritizes.
4. Real artifacts make claims believable: screens, projects, live critique, behind-the-scenes, workflow, experiment.
5. Service selling is usually indirect in the body; content earns the inference "this person can solve this."
6. Topic specificity beats generic expertise.
7. Strong creators make their thinking observable, not only the final answer.
8. Buyer meaning comes before implementation language.
