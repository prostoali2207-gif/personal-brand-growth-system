## Purpose

It is a high-precision benchmark set for studying how strong service-business creators:
- choose content topics;
- frame buyer problems;
- speak on camera / in narration;
- demonstrate expertise;
- connect useful content to commercial demand;
- avoid sounding like generic "I offer X/Y/Z" advertising.

## Selection method

Required evidence mix:
- current/recent activity;
- sustained audience or strong repeated distribution;
- real service/operator credibility;
- relevance to websites/design/creative services/automation;
- observable commercial path;
- creator craft;
- transferability to Ali's offer.

## Cross-benchmark patterns worth testing

These are observations/hypotheses, not universal rules.

### P1 — Topic starts from buyer situation
Top commercially relevant examples rarely open with:
"I build X."

They more often open with:
- a mistake;
- a buyer question;
- a costly misunderstanding;
- a strong claim;
- a visible problem;
- a before/after decision;
- a real process.

### P2 — Expertise is demonstrated through judgment
The creator explains:
- what they noticed;
- what decision they made;
- why the obvious/default approach is weak;
- what they would do instead.

This is stronger than a feature tour.

### P3 — Real work is proof, not the whole topic
Projects appear as:
- example;
- case;
- screen proof;
- process evidence;
- "here is what happened".

The post remains about a buyer-relevant idea.

### P4 — Technical depth is layered
Strong service content does not assume buyer fluency.
Technical detail enters only when it increases:
- trust;
- differentiation;
- proof;
- decision quality.

### P5 — Authorial point of view matters
High-quality creators do not merely explain correct information.
They have a recognizable judgment:
- what they reject;
- what they prioritize;
- what they believe is misunderstood.

### P6 — Content-to-offer continuity
The content topic naturally connects to the kind of problem the creator is paid to solve.
The sales path is coherent without turning each video into an ad.
